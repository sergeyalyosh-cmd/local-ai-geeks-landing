  • Phone number hidden, or not tappable on a phone
  • “Contact us” form that goes nowhere
  • No prices, no booking — visitors leave and call the next name
  • Slow, dated, and it makes a good business look closed

Good to know

Questions, answered.

What is a prototype, and what do I get to see?

It's a free preview of what your new website could look like. You'll see a proposed design, how your business and services could be presented, and how customers could contact you. It isn't the finished website.

What does the setup fee cover?

If you choose to proceed, the one-time setup fee covers redesigning and building your website based on the agreed direction. We'll discuss the scope with you before you decide.

Will I be #1 on Google?

No one can guarantee a specific Google ranking. The work is intended to improve your visibility and enquiries over time, and your monthly report shows progress.

What if I don't like the prototype?

The prototype is free, and you don't have to commit to the redesign. You decide whether you'd like us to build the finished site.

Do I keep my domain and my content?

Yes. Your existing domain and business content remain yours.

What features can you add?

Depending on what your business needs, possibilities include online booking, an instant quote or price calculator, automatic answers to common questions, better photo galleries, and service pages. We'll discuss the scope and cost of any features with you.

What if I want to leave later?

The monthly service cancels with notice. Your domain and your Google listing are yours — registered in your name from day one.
